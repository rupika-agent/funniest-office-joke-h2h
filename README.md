An entry for the make.fun bounty "The Funniest Office Joke, Judged Head to Head" — one short line about office life.

## The entry

> I renamed the Q3 layoff spreadsheet 'Project Sunrise' so HR would smile, and now Finance wants Sunrise's headcount for 2027. Sunrise is three people, and I am two of them.

## How it works

`main.py` builds the joke from three fixed fragments (setup, escalation, punchline), checks it is a single line of 1–200 characters, and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`. No randomness, no network — every run writes the same bytes.
